=====================================================
GLOBAL ENTERPRISE OPERATING POLICIES & SOP MANUAL (2026)
=====================================================

[SECTION 1: REMOTE WORK & WORKSPACE STIPENDS]
1.1 Eligibility: All full-time employees who have completed the 90-day probationary period are eligible for remote work arrangements. 
1.2 Home Office Setup Allowance: The company provides a one-time setup reimbursement of up to $1,200. This fund covers ergonomic chairs, desks, and external monitors. Receipts must be submitted to the Finance portal under category CODE-REMOTE-99 within 30 days of purchase.
1.3 Monthly Connectivity Stipend: Eligible remote staff receive a monthly internet subsidy of $75 credited directly to payroll.

[SECTION 2: HEALTH, FITNESS & WELLNESS PERKS]
2.1 Wellness Reimbursement Fund: Full-time personnel can claim up to $500 per calendar year. This benefit covers gym memberships, fitness trackers, and registered yoga classes.
2.2 Claims Procedure: Employees must upload proof of active membership. Payouts are distributed on the last Friday of each month under budget tag CODE-FIT-302.

[SECTION 3: DATA SECURITY & HARDWARE COMPLIANCE]
3.1 Machine Encryption: All company-issued laptops must enforce BitLocker or FileVault full-disk encryption with a minimum 256-bit AES key.
3.2 Incident Reporting Protocol (Lost Devices): In the event of a lost device or suspected security breach, personnel must notify the Security Operations Center (SOC) immediately via emergency hotline EXT-9110 or by triggering alert code SEC_BREACH_CRITICAL in the IT portal within 1 hour.
3.3 Password Policy: System credentials must be at least 14 characters long, contain at least one special character, and expire every 90 days.
3.4 Hardware Refresh Cycle: Standard developer laptops are eligible for an upgrade every 36 months. Request code for hardware refresh is CODE-HW-REFRESH.

[SECTION 4: BUSINESS TRAVEL & EXPENSES]
4.1 Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem): When traveling on corporate business, employees are allocated a daily per diem of $85 for domestic trips and $130 for international trips. Alcohol expenses are strictly non-reimbursable unless pre-approved by a VP.
4.2 Flight Bookings: Economy class is mandatory for all flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted only for non-stop flights exceeding 6 continuous hours under booking code CODE-AIR-BIZ.
4.3 Hotel Accommodations: Maximum nightly hotel rates are capped at $250 for Tier 1 cities (New York, London, Tokyo) and $150 for Tier 2 cities. 

[SECTION 5: PAID TIME OFF (PTO) & LEAVE]
5.1 Annual Accrual: Employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month, totaling 18 days per year. A maximum of 5 unused days may roll over into the next calendar year.
5.2 Bereavement Leave: The company grants up to 5 days of fully paid leave for the loss of an immediate family member.
5.3 Sick Leave: Employees are allotted 7 paid sick days annually. A doctor's note is required if an employee takes more than 3 consecutive sick days.

[SECTION 6: SOCIAL MEDIA & PUBLIC COMMUNICATIONS]
6.1 Speaking Engagements: Any employee speaking at an external conference must have their slide deck approved by the PR department at least 14 days in advance.
6.2 Confidentiality: Employees are strictly prohibited from sharing unreleased product roadmaps, client names, or internal financial metrics on any social media platform.
